Research

Seminar paper · 2026

Multi-stage waste detection using YOLOv12 and hierarchical multi-label classification

My undergraduate thesis and seminar paper. I built a two-stage pipeline where YOLOv12 first detects waste objects, then a hierarchical classification network assigns fine-grained labels across three levels of a waste hierarchy.

Trained and evaluated on a custom dataset of thousands of annotated waste images, the detection stage reaches an F1-score of 0.84 while the classification stage reaches 0.97 mAP.
